# Assemble the Incident Bundle on the Collection Workstation

Each Remote Node Collector emits a transient gzip-compressed Node Evidence Archive over SSH instead of persisting a remote archive, while the Collection Workstation receives the complete stream and admits its structure before extraction. The workstation alone combines admitted source contributions with inventory, Kubernetes, and Prometheus evidence into the final Incident Bundle, keeping the deliverable under operator control and preventing untrusted or incomplete private staging from entering the archive. The Incident Bundle Module consumes the completed workstation workspace as one terminal lifecycle: after streaming admitted evidence into an unpublished candidate, it attempts exact workspace cleanup, records that result in final metadata, closes the archive, and only then publishes the final name. Because first-version recoverable two-pass packaging is deferred, a later candidate-close or publication failure may leave no deliverable after the copied workspace evidence has been removed; original source evidence is never deleted by this trade-off.
